取消文化与可持续发展目标

An assessment of ‘Cancel Culture’ in relation to Sustainable Development

Full Text Tian, Yiwen*
Affiliation: *Yunnan University

Abstracts

教育工作者和教育学家在塑造人们对取消文化的理解和利用方式发挥着至关重要的作用。他们可以通过以下方式与取消文化进行互动，将其转化为对社会积极有益的形式。首先，鼓励对取消文化现象进行批判性分析，而不是盲目地支持或谴责。教育者可以帮助学生理解取消文化的复杂性、起源和影响，培养他们具备批判性思维的能力。其次，创造安全和包容的环境，促进对取消文化进行开放性对话和讨论。鼓励学生表达他们对取消文化的看法、关注和经验，引导他们进行尊重的辩论和思想交流，让不同的观点得以听到和理解。再者，强调取消文化行为的道德考量和道德意义，促使学生反思取消文化背后的伦理原则和价值观，如问责、正义和同理心。通过培养学生对取消文化何时何地适用与不适用的细腻理解，帮助他们形成对取消文化的深入见解。此外，赋予学生积极变革的能力，在取消文化的动态中成为积极的变革者。鼓励他们倡导解决社会问题和促进社会正义的建设性方法，教导他们如何以培养同理心、对话和理解为基础从事行动主义、倡导和联盟。最后，培养学生媒体素养，以批判性评估与取消文化相关的信息和消息。教育他们关于媒体、社交媒体平台和在线社区在塑造取消文化叙事中的作用，让他们具备辨别虚假信息、避免信息茧房和理性参与数字话语的能力。强调多样性、平等和包容的重要性，引导学生认识到在解决社会不公正问题时，多元视角和交叉视角的重要性，并促进他们认识到社会变革和进步所需的多元参与。 Educators and scholars are pivotal in shaping the understanding and impact of cancel culture, turning it into a force for social good. They can begin by fostering critical analysis rather than uncritical support or condemnation. By helping students grasp the complexity, origins, and consequences of cancel culture, educators enhance their critical thinking abilities. Creating a safe and inclusive space for open dialogue about cancel culture is also essential. Educators should encourage students to share their perspectives and engage in respectful debates, ensuring diverse viewpoints are heard and considered. Additionally, emphasizing the moral dimensions of cancel culture—such as accountability, justice, and empathy—can guide students in reflecting on its ethical implications and determining when it is appropriate or not. Empowering students to become proactive agents of change within cancel culture is crucial. Educators should advocate for constructive approaches to social issues and social justice, teaching students to engage in activism and advocacy grounded in empathy and understanding. Moreover, fostering media literacy is vital for students to critically assess cancel culture-related information. Educators should instruct students on the roles of media, social media platforms, and online communities in shaping cancel culture narratives, equipping them to identify misinformation, avoid echo chambers, and participate thoughtfully in digital discourse.
