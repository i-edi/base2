I-EDI Global Research Award: SDG 10

The Institute for Equality in Development Innovations (I-EDI.org) is dedicated to supporting young student leaders in equality research.
This award recognizes student research, essays, and papers broadly related to SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities, with the goal of promoting equitable life and human development across the globe.

At-a-Glance

  • Open to postsecondary students worldwide
  • Accepts essays, academic papers, and research writing
  • Past writing and previously completed papers are permitted
  • Winning student research will receive $1000 USD
  • Multiple submissions are accepted

Do I Have to Write Something New?

No. I-EDI aims to recognize student ideas that may not have previously received the platform they deserve.

Students are welcome to submit:

  • Previous essays
  • Past research papers
  • Current independent projects

However, submissions must meet one important requirement: The idea must be developed by youth either independently or in youth-led teams. Past or current research connected to a thesis or dissertation led by academic supervisors will not be considered youth-led.


What Can I Submit?

Students may submit academic writing; research papers; personal writing; or their past written work.
Submissions must:

  • Be written in English
  • Be over 3000 words
  • Be youth-led work
  • Fictional submissions are not accepted. However, I-EDI strongly welcomes student research analyzing fiction; youth media; digital culture; media impacts on society


I Want to Write Something New. What Can I Write About?

Acknowledging the interdisciplinarity of the Sustainable Development Goals, I-EDI welcomes submissions broadly related to equality.

Your research may be related to health; education; environment; technology; social justice; community and non-profit work. We also welcome youth submissions related to media, especially on youth media's impacts on mental health, and social behavioral outcomes


Can I Submit More Than One Paper?

Yes. Multiple submissions are accepted.


Award Value

Winning student research will receive $1000 USD


Accessibility & Equity Support

Historically, I-EDI has dedicated funds to supporting students with:

  1. Disabilities
  2. Life-limiting health conditions
  3. Foster or youth-in-care backgrounds
  4. Survivor-of-abuse status

How to Apply

Email Submission

Submit all .doc or .docx files to: iedi.generations [at] gmail.com and amy [at] i-iedi.org using your university, school, or institutional email address. This is intended to verify student identity, as I-EDI collects no other student ID etc.

OR use the I-EDI Internal Application Form

Final Checks

  • Applicants must be postsecondary students
  • Student research must be submitted in English
  • Multiple submissions and past writing are eligible, as long as over 3000 words in length and student-led
  • AI-assisted writing is permitted, but usage must be declared
