2030 Strategic Plan At-a-Glance

Equality Grants

  • What I-EDI Offers: $1,000~10,000 USD grants which address strategic focus
  • Focus: patients experiencing cancer and other life-limiting illness; severe disability, including mental health disability; foster/youth-in care; prevention of abuse
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted; individuals accepted alongside organizations
  • Competitive Criteria: Projects must clearly document community-level impact of funding
  • How to Apply: LOI 2-page max addressed to Amy Smithe, amy [at] i-edi.org

24-HR Support Community [24SC]

  • What I-EDI Offers: Global, online, group-based, <24 hour response time guaranteed. Peer emotional support for youth, patients, and caregivers
  • Focus: Crisis, loneliness & mental health, peer community.
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted; currently, we are only able to offer support in English though peers may offer additional language skills
  • How to Apply: No formal application required; contact I-EDI directly here via the new official Platform.

Innovation Fellows

  • What I-EDI Offers: Full room and board renewable annually, with opportunities to lead new program
  • Focus: this unique fellowship aims to support individuals aspiring towards long-term philanthropy without financial reward - with I-EDI helping to remove financial insecurity
  • Eligibility: individuals over the age of 18 with work or volunteering experience
  • Competitive Criteria: This is a highly-selective program for individuals committed to longer-term philanthropy in addition to minimalist living comparable to our international development workers. Applicants may instead refer to I-EDI's available internships; research training; student grants; leaders & fellows for retired professionals; or volunteer team
  • How to Apply: Applicants should send introductory email to amy [at] i-edi.org for application procedures

I-EDI Secondary School Diploma Support [SSPS]

  • What I-EDI Offers: fully-personalized, online schooling towards official Canadian high school diploma via our partners
  • Focus: Youth experiencing school interruptions due to cancer and other life-limiting illness; severe disability, including mental health disability; foster/youth-in care; survivor of trauma
  • Eligibility: Due to the nature of the secondary school diploma, SSPS is currently limited to youth with Canadian or US citizenship
  • How to Apply: No formal application required; contact I-EDI directly here via the new official Platform to discuss how we can serve you best.

I-EDI Research Training

  • What I-EDI Offers: training for students interested in publishing a full peer-reviewed journal article
  • Focus: proposed research should be broadly related to equality, for example, in medicine; law; health; income; education; environmental sustainability; technology; media; communicaions among other human development subjects.
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted; English skills required
  • How to Apply: No formal application required; contact I-EDI directly here via the new official Platform to discuss how we can serve you best.

Allied Health Patient Care [AHPC]

  • What I-EDI Offers: Both 1-on-1 personalized support for complex symptoms management, fully-funded, offered by health promotion researchers and registered TCM care providers
  • Focus: preventative health and complex symptom case-analysis for patients who have not found solutions in their existing health care arrangements
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted in English for online support
  • How to Apply: Please connect with our team here via the new official Platform to discuss how we can serve you best.

Student Interns Program

  • What I-EDI Offers: 1-on-1, tailored internship experiences resulting in peer-reviewed evidence of student impact
  • Focus: We welcome all students and recent graduates to reach out and contribute to I-EDI's existing programs in research; health; education; and social justice.
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted in English
  • Competitive Criteria: Individuals must be able to complete tasks both independently and as part of a team. Please note that I-EDI internships are not unpaid opportunities, though highly-qualified applicants may be eligible for the Innovation Fellowship
  • How to Apply: Applicants should send CV to amy [at] i-edi.org for interview.

Equitable & Just Help Program [EJH]

  • What I-EDI Offers: 24-hour response time to overcome challenges not currently covered by I-EDI services
  • Focus: In recognizing research on systemic gaps - especially those affecting marginalized communities - I-EDI responds to queries which constitute unmet needs in human development
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted in English; preference is given to patients experiencing cancer and other life-limiting illness; severe disability, including mental health disability; foster/youth-in care; survivors of trauma
  • How to Apply: No application needed; contact I-EDI directly here via the new official Platform.

Life & Revival [L&R]

  • What I-EDI Offers: Flexible options for fully-anonymous or weekly group voice conference, focusing on discussions and explorations into spirituality
  • Focus: In recognizing how spiritual care may both confound and alleviate challenging experiences, I-EDI offers direct mentorship from individuals experiencing both sides to guide those in search of answers
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted in English
  • How to Apply: No application needed; contact I-EDI directly here via the new official Platform.

Executive Retired Professionals [XRP]

  • What I-EDI Offers: flexible, focused, meaningful leadership positions that mobilize existing skills for maximum social impact
  • Focus: All position queries are welcome, and we invite all retired professionals to discuss how I-EDI's equality-oriented work with communities can help you maximize your potential and reach
  • Eligibility: Global unrestricted in English
  • How to Apply: Please connect with us through an introductory email at amy [at] i-edi.org
